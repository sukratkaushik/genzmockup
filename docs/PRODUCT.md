# Product

## One line
AI lifestyle mockups for Gen Z sellers: upload a flat design, get listing-ready on-model photos in seconds.

## Users and use cases
1. **POD / e-commerce sellers (primary).** Problem: lifestyle photos for every new tee or mug are expensive and slow. Solution: upload a PNG, get market-ready photos for Shopify, Etsy or TikTok Shop.
2. **Social media marketers and brand managers.** Problem: constant fresh, demographic-targeted content for Instagram and TikTok. Solution: demographic styling plus the collage maker for quick promo posts.
3. **Apparel and graphic designers.** Problem: clients cannot picture a flat design on a garment. Solution: on-model concept shots in dynamic lighting for pitches.

## Feature set (current, verified)
- Upload: drag and drop flat design.
- Products (9): t-shirt, oversized tee, hoodie, sweatshirt, mug, phone case, tote bag, notebook, pillow. Colour palettes for Gildan 5000 / 18500 / 18000.
- Gen Z styling: 10 poses, 10 actions, 8 expressions, 10 locations, 5 lighting setups, 8 accessories.
- Surprise Me mode, quality presets, saved presets.
- Gallery (max 100), collage maker (max 9), bulk ZIP export.
- Dashboard: today and lifetime counts, success / failure rate, cost metrics (hardcoded estimate).
- Undo / redo, dark / light mode, sidebar nav.

## Feature backlog (ordered by seller value)
1. Marketplace export presets (Etsy listing sizes, Shopify, TikTok Shop 1:1 and 9:16).
2. Batch: one design across many products and vibes in one run.
3. Listing helper: title, tags, description from the design and mockup.
4. Brand kit: saved colours, logo, preferred vibes.
5. Social templates: carousel and story layouts from the collage tool.
6. Consistent model / "brand model" across a collection.
7. Direct publish (Etsy / Shopify) once API approvals allow.

## Success metrics
- Activation: first mockup generated within 5 minutes of signup.
- Retention: sellers generating in 3 of the first 4 weeks.
- Revenue: free to paid conversion; monthly recurring revenue.
- Quality: mockup keep rate (downloaded / generated).

## Risks
- Gemini output may distort fine print or logos. Needs real-seller testing and clear expectations.
- Competing with large incumbents on general mockups; we win on niche taste and workflow.
- Rights and policy questions around AI imagery of people and marketplace disclosure rules (check Etsy and TikTok Shop AI policies).
