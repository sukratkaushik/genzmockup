# Go-to-market

Distribution is the founder's weakest area, so this is planned like engineering.

## Principle
Sell where Gen Z sellers already are, and let the product's output be the marketing: every mockup is a shareable, on-trend image.

## Phase 1: First 10 users (weeks 1-3)
- Find sellers in Etsy seller forums, Reddit (r/printondemand, r/EtsySellers), Facebook POD groups, TikTok seller creators.
- Offer a free done-with-you run: they send a design, you send back mockups. Ask for feedback and a quote.
- Record which vibes and products they pick. That is your roadmap.

## Phase 2: Content engine (weeks 3-10)
- Short-form videos (TikTok, Reels, Shorts): "flat design to lifestyle photo in 10 seconds" screen recordings.
- Weekly "design of the week" before/after posts.
- SEO pages per product and vibe (hoodie mockup, mug mockup, etc.).

## Phase 3: Loops
- Free-tier watermark with a link back.
- Referral credits (extra mockups for both sides).
- Public gallery or share pages for generated sets.
- Partnerships with POD design creators and course sellers.

## Metrics
Visitors to waitlist, waitlist to activated, activated to paid, content views per signup, CAC by channel.

## Pricing tests
Test Pro at €12, €19 and €29 against real cost per image; test credit packs against monthly plans.
